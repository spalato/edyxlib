TA: average scans and correct time zero¶

Example on 250709, scans s40 and s44. It reproduces the lab's s4044.dat (check at the end).

Steps: read, inspect, average, subtract the pre-t0 spectrum, shift by the t0 curve, save.

Not shown: shot rejection and t0 picking, which are interactive (they will live in pyspan). Here no shot is rejected, and the t0 curve is the one measured on s20-s27.

Average and subtract the pre-t0 spectrum¶

In [8]:
avg = avg_scans(dod)
neg_spec = avg_spectra(avg[..., t < t_pre])  # mean spectrum before time zero, saved below
avg_sub = subtract_pre_t0(t, avg, t_pre)    # avg - neg_spec

fig, axes = plot_maps(t, wl, [avg, avg_sub], titles=["average", "pre-t0 subtracted"])

Time-zero correction¶

The stored curve is sampled on the same wavelengths: columns are (wl, t0). shift_trace moves the signal at t0[i] to delay 0, and keeps only the delays valid for every wavelength.

In [21]:
t0_corr = np.load(t0_file)["t0_corr"]
assert np.allclose(t0_corr[:, 0], wl), "t0 curve sampled on other wavelengths"
t0 = t0_corr[:, 1]

t_c, shifted = shift_trace(t, avg_sub, t0, edges="clip")
