TA: average scans and correct time zero¶

Example on 250709, scans s40 and s44. It reproduces the lab's s4044.dat (check at the end).

Steps: read, inspect, average, subtract the pre-t0 spectrum, shift by the t0 curve, save.

Not shown: shot rejection and t0 picking, which are interactive (they will live in pyspan). Here no shot is rejected, and the t0 curve is the one measured on s20-s27.

Legacy t0 file¶

The t0 curve of s20-s27 is stored in the old _t0corr.npz format. It is converted once to a calibration file; new notebooks write one directly with write_calibration. The points were picked on the measured wavelengths, hence axis="wl_raw". The fit is kept for information.

In [3]:
legacy = np.load(data_dir / "s2027_t0corr.npz")
t0_file.parent.mkdir(exist_ok=True)
write_calibration(
    t0_file, "t0", "wl_raw", *legacy["t0_corr"].T, units=["nm", "ps"],
    date=datetime.date(2025, 7, 9), source="s20-s27", made_by="s2027_t0corr.npz (converted)",
    fit={"model": "polynomial in eV, low order first", "coef": legacy["pol_coeff"],
         "points": legacy["t0_meas"]},
)

Read¶

stack_traces checks that all files share the same delays and wavelengths. Axes of dod: (file, wl, delay). NaN become masked points, so they are ignored by the averages.

Apply the wavelength calibration right after reading, so every later step uses the calibrated axis: wl = interp_calibration(read_calibration(wl_file), wl). Not here: the lab's output is on the measured axis.

fig, axes = plot_maps(t, wl, [avg, avg_sub], titles=["average", "pre-t0 subtracted"])

Time-zero correction¶

The t0 curve is evaluated at the data wavelengths, by value. A wavelength outside the range of the curve raises an error (outside="error", the default). shift_trace moves the signal at t0[i] to delay 0, and keeps only the delays valid for every wavelength.

In [7]:
t0 = interp_calibration(read_calibration(t0_file), wl)

t_c, shifted = shift_trace(t, avg_sub, t0)
